## How one row can finish before the next starts

- R01 proves the API denial boundary, not the completed staff desk. Later account rows prove allowed desk behaviour with real production identity.
- R04 includes the smallest secure request form and case display necessary to prove its own sentence. R13 adds the consolidated today experience. No temporary unauthenticated desk is permitted.
- R05–R09 include their minimum role landing/denial screens. R14 finishes signing; it cannot be claimed passed merely because R08 proves case reading.
- R11 includes the minimum test checkout, refund control and case payment view needed to run its proof. R16 closes remaining navigation and parity gaps rather than postponing essential payment UI.
- Source/version capture, a pre-change backup and a known rollback are required from R01. R19–R23 assemble and independently prove production readiness; they do not defer basic safety until the end.
- If an earlier acceptance genuinely cannot pass without unrelated later work, correct the row boundary **before FREEZE-PLAN-01**. After freeze, stop on failure and preserve the frozen order. New ideas go to parking. Any necessary scope clarification is explicit and versioned; it does not move another row ahead or bypass the failed proof.

## Prerequisites before affected real actions

R23 assembles evidence; it does not postpone authority until the end. Before the first actual placement/referral offer, commission promise, charge or binding signature, the affected row must establish the correct entity/authority, listing permission, accepted scope/terms, fee purpose/payee/custody and approved document. Open legal questions are not approvals. Use clearly marked synthetic accounts, bills, receipts and nonbinding document fixtures for technical proofs; they cannot be represented as actual transactions or production document approval. Production-effective templates and terms must be approved before real signature or admission. The exact commercial card remains unsigned until R18 ACCEPT.

R11/R16 retain payer, payee, currency, amount, fee type, processor/mode, custody model, invoice number, receipt, refund policy, dispute/reconciliation state and manual-change approver. Refunds include reason, approver, amount and payee. This applies the existing money law without adding a new accounting product.

The original R01 invariant still applies: pin current deployed inputs, relevant configuration and permissions; retain a recoverable pre-change copy; demonstrate the way back before mutation. Resolve actual store bindings before a store-affecting change. Failure stops that row, not an invitation to replace Core or diagnose via real family data.
